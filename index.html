Welcome to SiratKids

A Sacred Space of Learning

Welcome to a sacred space of learning designed to cultivate a deep, authentic, and lifelong connection to Islam. Our mission is to provide children and families with a structured, beautifully designed curriculum rooted firmly in the Quran and the authentic Sunnah, according to the understanding of the righteous predecessors (Salaf us-Salih).

We believe that Islamic education is not merely about memorizing rules; it is about understanding before memorization. By connecting the mind to sound intellect and the heart to sincere devotion, we help the next generation navigate the modern world with an unwavering Islamic identity.

One Unified Whole

Our Core Subjects

Our curriculum is deliberately integrated so that students see Islam as a living, unified whole.

⭐

Aqeedah & Tawheed

Building a sound, pure belief system: who Allah is, His beautiful Names and Attributes, and the pillars of faith — protected by clear, logical, textual proofs.
💧

Fiqh (Worship)

The practical rulings of worship: performing Wudu and Salah with love and precision, and understanding the wisdom behind fasting and charity.
📖

Hadith

Connecting children directly to the words of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ through selected authentic narrations, extracting moral lessons and daily manners.
📜

Seerah

Bringing the life of the Prophet ﷺ and his noble Companions to life — his character, mercy, and struggles — making him the ultimate role model.
🛖

Adhkar & Manners

Daily supplications and Prophetic etiquette — from waking and sleeping to eating and greeting — practised until they become character.
📚

Quran Reader

Read, listen, and explore the full Mushaf with word-by-word recitation, translations, tafsir, and tajweed highlighting.
