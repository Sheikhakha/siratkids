آدَابُ الطَّعَامِ وَالشُّرْبِ Eating & Drinking Manners

Good character and everyday etiquette from the Sunnah.

Manners
Eating Manners
1
Wash Hands غَسْلُ الْيَدَيْنِ قَبْلَ الطَّعَامِ Washing both hands before eating
→
2
Say Bismillah قَوْلُ "بِسْمِ اللهِ" قَبْلَ الْأَكْلِ Saying Bismillah before eating
→
3
Right Hand الْأَكْلُ بِالْيَمِينِ Eating with the right hand
→
4
Eating Mat الْأَكْلُ عَلَى السُّفْرَةِ Eating on a dining mat / cloth on the floor
→
5
Sit Humbly الْجُلُوسُ تَوَاضُعًا بِغَيْرِ إِتِّكَاءٍ Sitting humbly without leaning
→
