Our Core Subjects
Our curriculum is deliberately integrated so that students do not study these sciences in isolation, but rather see Islam as a living, unified whole [Source URL: https://nooruttawheed.com/academics/islamic-studies].

Aqeedah & Tawheed (Creed & Monotheism): Building a sound, pure belief system. We teach children who Allah is, His beautiful Names and Attributes, and the pillars of faith, protecting them from doubts with clear, logical, and textual proofs [Source URL: https://quranicma.com/islamic-studies, https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
Fiqh (Islamic Jurisprudence): The practical rulings of worship. From learning how to perform Wudu and Salah with love and precision, to understanding the wisdom behind fasting and charity [Source URL: https://quranicma.com/islamic-studies].
Hadith (Prophetic Traditions): Connecting children directly to the words of the Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ). We study selected, authentic narrations (such as Imam Nawawi’s collection) to extract moral lessons and daily manners (Adab) [Source URL: https://alimaanonline.co.uk/islamic-studies-program-level-1, https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
Seerah (Prophetic Biography): Bringing the life of the Prophet (ﷺ) and his noble Companions to life. We teach his character, his mercy, and his struggles, turning him into the ultimate role model for our children [Source URL: https://quranicma.com/islamic-studies, https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
Detailed Level Breakdown
Here is an overview of what you will find when you select each level from the dropdown menu:

🌱 Pre-Stage: Seeds of Faith (Ages 4–6)
Focus: Love, Familiarity, and Wonder [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum]
At this tender age, we do not overwhelm children with complex theological terminology. Instead, we focus on building a warm, emotional bond with Allah and His Messenger (ﷺ) [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].

Aqeedah: "Who is Allah?" – Allah is the Creator, the Merciful, Who made the beautiful world and loves us [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
Fiqh: Introduction to the beauty of prayer and cleanliness in a joyful, encouraging manner.
Seerah & Hadith: Simple, engaging stories of the Prophet’s (ﷺ) kindness to children, animals, and family [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
🌿 Stage 1: Foundations of Islam (Ages 7–9)
Focus: Structured Basics and Daily Practice
As children enter the age of discernment, we introduce structured learning with clear, authentic evidences.

Aqeedah: The Six Pillars of Iman (Belief in Allah, His Angels, His Books, His Messengers, the Last Day, and Divine Decree) explained simply.
Fiqh: Step-by-step guide to Wudu and the five daily prayers, emphasizing consistency and focus (Khushu).
Hadith: Memorizing short, impactful Prophetic sayings about honesty, kindness to parents, and good manners [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
Seerah: A chronological journey through the early life of the Prophet (ﷺ) in Makkah [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
🌳 Stage 2: Developing Devotion (Ages 10–12)
Focus: Understanding the "Why" behind the Law [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum]
Pre-teens begin to ask deeper questions. We address their intellectual curiosity with sound, textual proofs from authentic sources.

Aqeedah: Deepening the understanding of Tawheed (Tawheed ar-Rububiyyah, al-Uluhiyyah, and al-Asma' was-Sifat) using clear examples.
Fiqh: The rulings of fasting (Sawm), purification, and an introduction to the concept of accountability (Takleef).
Hadith: Studying selected narrations from Imam Nawawi's Forty Hadith, focusing on their practical application in modern daily life [Source URL: https://alimaanonline.co.uk/islamic-studies-program-level-1, https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
Seerah: The Hijrah (migration) to Madinah, the establishment of the Muslim community, and the noble character of the Sahabah [Source URL: https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
🏔️ Stage 3: Youth & Clarity (Ages 13+)
Focus: Critical Thinking, Identity, and Classical Texts [Source URL: https://nooruttawheed.com/academics/islamic-studies]
Preparing young adults to confidently practice and defend their faith in a complex world.

Aqeedah: Guided introductions to classical Sunni creed texts (such as Al-Aqeedah Al-Wasitiyyah or Al-Aqeedah Al-Tahawiyyah), addressing contemporary doubts with intellectual rigor [Source URL: https://nooruttawheed.com/academics/islamic-studies, https://madinahmedia.com/blog/stepbystep-islamic-studies-curriculum].
Fiqh: Contemporary issues facing Muslim youth, purification of the heart, and the Fiqh of daily transactions.
Hadith: An introduction to Hadith sciences (Mustalah al-Hadith) to understand how the sayings of the Prophet (ﷺ) were preserved with absolute scientific precision [Source URL: https://nooruttawheed.com/academics/islamic-studies].
Seerah & History: The final years of the Prophet (ﷺ), the era of the Rightly Guided Caliphs, and the profound contributions of Islamic civilization [Source URL: https://alimaanonline.co.uk/islamic-studies-program-level-1, https://nooruttawheed.com/academics/islamic-studies].
